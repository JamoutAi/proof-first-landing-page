# The 17-section outline (plus the header)

Each section below lists **Job** (why it exists), **Proof** (what fills it), **Copy** (the formula) and **Watch for** (mistakes that got caught in review). Sections marked **(B)** belong to the pain-first variant but work in any page that has the proof for them. Sections marked **OPTIONAL** depend on media or proof the owner may not have. Delete any section you can't fill with real material. Three sections have a no-photo fallback in the template instead: the hero, Meet the founder and the final call to action.

## 0. Header
- **Job:** Brand recognition and one escape-proof call to action.
- **Content:** Logo on the left. On the right, the rating ("5.0 on [platform]") plus a small primary button.
- **Ad pages:** No navigation menu. Every link that leaves the page lowers conversion.
- **Main site pages:** The logo links home. Site links go in the footer, not the header.

## 1. Hero
- **Job:** In five seconds, answer who it's for, what changes for them, how fast, and what to click.
- **Text column:**
  - Pill eyebrow: the audience or offer ("For busy small business owners · [Offer] on [Platform]").
  - H1 with one emphasized phrase. Angle A names the outcome. Angle B names the burden.
  - Lede in 2 or 3 sentences: who, outcome, timeframe, support ("with live help every week").
  - Primary button, plus a "Watch the 2-minute tour" link that scrolls to the video and plays it.
  - Micro line: price · time per day · cancel policy.
  - Proof row: 4 to 6 real member faces in an overlapping stack, stars, and "Rated 5.0 on [platform]".
- **Visual column, choose one:**
  - A founder cutout inside a brand-colored arch or rounded shape, with a soft decorative element behind it.
  - A real working photo (desk, laptop, product) in a rounded frame. This suits Angle B.
  - No usable photo yet: one strong member quote fills the arch instead (Option C in the template), with one highlighted phrase and the member's name and role.
- **Badges:** 3 or 4 sit in a grid directly UNDER the photo, never on it:
  - a credential ("Official [X] Partner")
  - a rating
  - something live ("Live this week, [session name]"), only if a real session is coming up
  - one short result quote with the member's photo
- **Watch for:**
  - Floating cards over the face (owners call them distracting).
  - Stock or AI photos.
  - A hero button that falls below the first phone screen. On phones, put the text first and the photo second.

## 2. Logo strip (OPTIONAL)
- **Job:** Borrowed credibility in one glance.
- **Proof:** Logos of organizations the founder trained, spoke for or delivered programs for. Recolor them to one ink color (`scripts/logo_mono.py`), then scroll them slowly in a marquee that pauses on hover.
- **Copy:** "[Name] has trained teams and audiences at".
- **Watch for:**
  - Client logos the owner can't show publicly. Ask.
  - Logos found in Downloads with no record of a real engagement. Leave them out unless the owner confirms.

## 3. Video tour (OPTIONAL)
- **Job:** Let them hear the founder. This is the biggest trust jump on the page.
- **No video?** Delete the section, and delete the hero's "Watch the tour" link with it.
- **Proof:** A 45 to 120 second founder video. It works best as a screen-recorded tour of the real community or program, with the founder's face picture-in-picture.
- **Layout:** Text on the left (eyebrow "The 2-minute tour, from me", headline, 3 bullets, button). Video on the right with a poster frame and a big play button.
- **Copy:** The bullets must match what the video actually says. Transcribe it first.
- **Watch for:**
  - Outdated lines inside the video (old trial offers, old prices, "you've been getting my emails" on a cold-traffic page). Trim them out.
  - Captions missing when the video has none burned in. Add a caption track.

## 4. Pain: fears and frustrations
- **Job:** Make the reader feel seen before you sell.
- **Layout:** 4 cards for Angle A, 6 cards for Angle B. Each card has an emoji, a bold one-line pain and one line of detail.
- **Copy:**
  - Mix frustrations ("You tried it and it sounded like a robot") with fears ("You're scared you're falling behind"). See `copy-playbook.md`.
  - Close with a short handwritten-style reassurance: "None of that is a you problem. It's a setup problem, and it's fixable in a week."
- **Watch for:** Shaming the reader. Name the pain, then take the blame off them.

## 5. Dream: Right now vs. With [offer] (B)
- **Job:** Dreams and desires, made concrete.
- **Layout:** A two-column table.
  - Left column, "Right now": muted text with ✕ marks.
  - Right column, "With [offer]": warm tint with ✦ marks.
  - 5 rows, then a handwritten line ("More time for clients. More time for family.") and a button ("I want that week →").
- **Copy:** Each right-hand row is a visible, felt outcome ("Replies drafted and waiting for your OK"), not a feature.

## 6. The plan
- **Job:** Make the first step feel small and certain.
- **Layout:** A grid of day or step cards. For a 7-day start that's 7 cards plus a highlighted "Day 8 and beyond" card.
- **Copy:** Start with a lede that removes the time fear ("each day takes about 20 minutes, made for busy owners"). Each card gets a 2 to 4 word title and one sentence.

## 7. The room: live sessions (OPTIONAL)
- **Job:** Prove it's a living community, not a video library.
- **Layout:**
  - A silent session reel inside a mock video-call window (traffic-light dots, "live call" label), with a caption chip that changes with each clip.
  - Two real photos beside it, framed in white with handwritten captions.
  - A button below.
- **Proof:** 5 to 8 moments from real recordings: a full gallery of faces, the founder teaching, a member demo slide, a "building it live" moment.
- **Watch for:**
  - Clips that show an old offer on screen. Crop it out.
  - Members' faces and names. The owner decides if that's OK.
  - Photo frames that don't fill their column (the figure margin bug).

## 8. Events calendar (B)
- **Job:** Answer "will I actually get help?" with specifics.
- **Proof:** The real calendar. Read 2 or 3 months to separate recurring events from one-offs.
- **Layout:** A 3-column card grid. Each card has a cadence chip (Weekly / Every other week / Monthly / VIP), the day and time, the event name, one line about it, and the host's avatar.
- **Copy:** End the lede with the time zone. Add "Busy week? Every session is recorded." only if every session really is recorded.

## 9. Platform screenshots (B)
- **Job:** "It's real, and here's the public page."
- **Proof:** Logged-out screenshots of the platform's public page (member count, rating, price, join button) and its reviews section.
- **Layout:** Two browser-frame mockups, slightly rotated. Each gets a caption and a "See the page on [platform] →" link.
- **Copy:** Round the member count down ("450+") so the text doesn't go stale, while the screenshot shows the exact number. Keep the rating and review count exact.

## 10. Wall of love
- **Job:** Overwhelming social proof, kept scannable.
- **Layout:** A masonry wall (CSS columns) mixing four card types:
  - **Result tiles:** a bold headline result, the quote with one phrase highlighted, and the person's role.
  - **Photo reviews:** avatar, name, role, stars (only if that review gave 5), the quote, and a tenure badge copied from what the platform shows ("Member for 10 months"). Don't guess at tenure or claim someone is still paying unless you can see it.
  - **Community posts, retyped:** low-res screenshots are unreadable at card size, so retype them word for word in a platform-style card with initials avatars and "Posted in [community]".
  - **One dark live-chat card:** 5 or 6 short chat messages from live sessions.
- **Rhythm:** Lead with the most emotional photo review, then alternate card types.
  - More than 12 cards: show 12 (8 on phones), then a "Show me more love" button.
  - 12 or fewer: show them all, and delete the button and the hide-on-phone classes.
- **Watch for:**
  - Doubled quote marks. The `q` element adds its own, so don't type quotes inside it.
  - Posts that mention health details or old offer terms. Leave those out.

## 11. Video testimonials (OPTIONAL)
- **Job:** Faces and voices of members.
- **Layout:** 3 to 5 vertical (9:16) cards in a row. On phones they become a swipe row. Each card has a poster, a play button, a name chip (unless the name is burned into the video), a hook headline in quotes, and one line about the person.
- **Proof:** Clips under 60 seconds. Trim the member's own calls to action ("link in my bio") and the interviewer's reactions.
- **Behavior:** Click to play with sound. Only one video plays at a time. Add a caption track when captions aren't burned in.

## 12. Meet the founder
- **Job:** Human connection plus authority.
- **Layout:**
  - Left: a photo collage of a stage shot, a working shot and a portrait, slightly rotated with white borders, plus a handwritten caption.
  - Right: the story in 2 or 3 short paragraphs, a handwritten sign-off, credential tags, and a certificates row with a verify link.
- **No photos yet:** use the fact card in the template instead of the collage. It holds the name, the credential, years of experience, the member count and the rating, all checkable.
- **Copy:**
  - Lead with empathy ("I know what it's like to be the one doing everything").
  - Then results ("thousands trained").
  - Then the credential, worded truthfully. Course certificates are "certified" or "completed", not "licensed".

## 13. Authority quotes (OPTIONAL)
- **Job:** Organizations vouch for the founder.
- **Layout:** A dark band over a darkened photo of the founder on stage, with 3 glass cards. Each card has the quote, plus a photo or initials, a name, a title and the organization. With no stage photo, use a plain dark band.
- **Proof:** Real quotes from organizers or clients who agreed to be named. No quotes, no section.

## 14. Price
- **Job:** Remove the surprise and frame the value.
- **Layout:** One centered card with a sticker ("Day 1 starts today", if the program really starts the day they join), a big price, the annual option, 5 benefit bullets, a button, and fine print (the real cancel policy, an upsell tier).
- **Copy:** Headline framing such as "Less than $2 a day to get your time back."

## 15. FAQ
- **Job:** Answer the six objections that stop the click.
- **The six:**
  - time
  - beginner level or "is it too late"
  - "what is [platform], is it a second purchase?"
  - extra costs (tools or subscriptions)
  - alternatives ("does it work with X?")
  - cancel policy (say "no call to talk you out of it" only if that's true)
- Keep the first one open by default.

## 16. Final call to action
- **Job:** One last emotional ask.
- **Layout:** A dark rounded band with text on the left and a full-body cutout of the founder on the right, with a soft light glow behind them (not a solid circle that matches their clothing).
- **No cutout yet:** use the "What happens next" card instead: three numbered steps, from joining to the first live session.
- **Copy:**
  - Headline: "Stop doing it all yourself. *Come on in.*" or "Your seat's saved. *Come on in.*"
  - One line restating price and cancel policy.
  - Button text that's a feeling ("Get my time back →").

## 17. Footer and sticky phone bar
- **Footer:**
  - Logo, tagline and legal links. Main pages also get site links.
  - A results disclaimer ("Member results are real and not typical or guaranteed").
  - A logo disclaimer ("Logos belong to their owners").
- **Sticky phone bar:** Appears after the hero scrolls away and hides while the price card is on screen. Shows the offer and price on the left and a button on the right.
